Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

The short version: Nyan Digest has no account, no server and no tracking of its own. The only thing that leaves your PC is what Claude needs to write a read.

What happens on your PC

  • Downloading audio, captions and video from YouTube (with yt-dlp), and reading your own files.
  • Transcribing speech with Whisper, and picking pictures from the video with ffmpeg.
  • Saving everything: transcripts and working files in the work folder, your reads in the reads folder, both inside the Nyan Digest folder (unless you chose another place for your reads).

What is sent to Claude

  • To write a read, Nyan Digest runs Claude Code on your PC, signed in with your own Claude account. Claude Code sends the transcript, the video's title and details, a few candidate pictures and your note (if you wrote one) to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude.
  • Anthropic handles that under its own terms and privacy policy, the same as when you use Claude yourself.

Other connections

  • YouTube, to get the videos you add.
  • Once a week, Python's package index, to keep the YouTube downloader (yt-dlp) up to date, because YouTube changes often.
  • The first time, Hugging Face, to download the Whisper speech model.
  • During setup only: Windows' winget, to install Python and ffmpeg, and Claude's installer if you ask for Claude Code.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. The company hosting it may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.
