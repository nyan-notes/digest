Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

The short version: Nyan Digest has no account, no server and no tracking of its own. The only thing that leaves your PC is what your AI writer needs to write a read.

What happens on your PC

  • Downloading audio, captions and video from YouTube (with yt-dlp), and reading your own files.
  • Transcribing speech with Whisper, and picking pictures from the video with ffmpeg.
  • Saving everything: transcripts and working files in the work folder, your reads in the reads folder, both inside the Nyan Digest folder (unless you chose another place for your reads).

What is sent to your AI writer

  • To write a read, Nyan Digest runs the writer you picked on your PC, with your own account: Claude Code (Anthropic), Gemini CLI (Google) or Codex (OpenAI). It sends the transcript, the video's title and details, a few candidate pictures and your note (if you wrote one) to that company.
  • That company handles it under its own terms and privacy policy, the same as when you use its AI yourself. With a free Gemini API key, Google may use what you send to improve its products, as its free-tier terms say.

Other connections

  • YouTube, to get the videos you add.
  • Once a week, Python's package index, to keep the YouTube downloader (yt-dlp) up to date, because YouTube changes often.
  • The first time, Hugging Face, to download the Whisper speech model.
  • During setup only: Windows' winget, to install Python, ffmpeg and Node.js, and the installer of the writer you picked (Claude's installer, or npm for Gemini CLI and Codex).

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. The company hosting it may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.
